## Introduction

Blogzy is a modern blogging platform that allows you to create, share, and discover blog posts. It features a clean, responsive design and a rich set of features to enhance your blogging experience.

### Key Features

- User authentication and profile management
- Rich text editor with Markdown support
- Tagging and categorization of posts
- Comments and interactions
- Search functionality
- Responsive design for all devices

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

You can access Blogzy through your web browser at:

- Production: https://blogzy.example.com
- Development: http://localhost:12000

### Creating an Account

1. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner
2. Fill in your details:
   - Username
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox

## User Authentication

### Logging In

1. Click on the "Log In" button in the top-right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Log In"

### Resetting Your Password

1. On the login page, click "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Click "Reset Password"
4. Check your email for a password reset link
5. Follow the link and create a new password

### Logging Out

1. Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Select "Log Out" from the dropdown menu

## Blog Management

### Creating a New Post

1. Click on the "New Post" button in the navigation bar
2. Enter a title for your post
3. Write your content using the rich text editor
   - You can use Markdown syntax for formatting
   - You can upload images by clicking the image icon
4. Add tags to categorize your post (separated by commas)
5. Select a category for your post
6. Preview your post by clicking "Preview"
7. Click "Publish" to make your post public, or "Save Draft" to save it for later

### Editing a Post

1. Navigate to the post you want to edit
2. Click the "Edit" button (only visible if you're the author)
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Update" to save your changes

### Deleting a Post

1. Navigate to the post you want to delete
2. Click the "Delete" button (only visible if you're the author)
3. Confirm the deletion

## User Profiles

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click on your username or profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu

### Editing Your Profile

1. Go to your profile page
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Update your information:
   - Profile picture
   - Display name
   - Bio
   - Social media links
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Viewing Your Posts

1. Go to your profile page
2. Your published posts will be displayed in the "Posts" tab
3. Your draft posts will be displayed in the "Drafts" tab

## Search and Discovery

### Searching for Posts

1. Click on the search icon in the navigation bar
2. Enter your search query
3. Press Enter or click the search icon

### Filtering Search Results

You can filter search results by:
- Date (newest, oldest)
- Relevance
- Author
- Tags
- Categories

### Discovering New Content

The home page features:
- Trending posts
- Recent posts
- Editor's picks
- Posts from authors you follow

## Comments and Interactions

### Commenting on a Post

1. Navigate to the post
2. Scroll to the comments section
3. Enter your comment in the text box
4. Click "Post Comment"

### Replying to Comments

1. Click "Reply" under the comment you want to respond to
2. Enter your reply
3. Click "Post Reply"

### Liking Posts and Comments

- Click the heart icon on a post or comment to like it
- Click again to unlike

### Sharing Posts

1. Click the share icon on a post
2. Choose your sharing method:
   - Copy link
   - Share to Twitter
   - Share to Facebook
   - Share to LinkedIn
   - Share via email

## Mobile Experience

Blogzy is fully responsive and works on all devices:

- The navigation menu collapses into a hamburger menu on small screens
- Images and text adapt to screen size
- Touch-friendly interface for mobile users

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Log In

- Make sure you're using the correct email and password
- Check if Caps Lock is on
- Try resetting your password

#### Can't Create or Edit Posts

- Make sure you're logged in
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page

#### Images Not Loading

- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
